## Official Selection

The official player design selected for the project is:

**Classic Box**

### Reason

Classic Box is the strongest option because it:

- best represents the ANDARE brand
- clearly communicates the "fresh pasta to go" concept
- is easy to recognize at a small pixel-art size
- is simple enough for a first playable version
- leaves room for future upgrades and alternate visual states

### Final Direction

The player ship will be a flying ANDARE takeout box with:

- visible pasta on top
- a clean front-facing box shape
- the ANDARE identity on the front
- two small thrusters underneath
- simple, readable arcade-style animations
